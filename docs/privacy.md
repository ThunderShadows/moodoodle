# moodoodle privacy policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

moodoodle is a browser extension that saves images you choose into a private collection on your computer.
We (the developer) run no servers, have no accounts and collect no analytics.

## What moodoodle stores, and where

When you press **Keep** (or "Keep image", or "Keep all on this page"), moodoodle stores in your browser's
local storage (IndexedDB), on your device only:

- the image file you chose to keep
- the address and title of the page it came from, and the image's own address
- the date you kept it, its size, its color palette, and any tags you add

This is "website content" and "web browsing activity" in Chrome Web Store terms. It is used only to show,
search and export your collection. It is never sent to us or anyone else, never sold, and never used for advertising.

To show the Keep button, moodoodle runs on the pages you visit and notices when your pointer is over an image.
Nothing about a page is stored or sent until you press Keep. Opening the popup counts the images on the
current page so it can offer "Keep all"; that count is not stored.

## When information leaves your browser

Only when you press **Find similar**: moodoodle opens Google Lens in a new tab with that image's address.
From then on, Google's privacy policy applies to that page.

## Your control

- Remove any image from the gallery at any time.
- Uninstalling moodoodle deletes everything it stored.
- If this policy changes, for example if paid features need an email address, we will tell you in the extension
  before anything new is collected.
