# moodoodle privacy policy

_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

moodoodle is a browser extension that saves images you choose into a private collection on your computer.
We (the developer) run no servers of our own and collect no analytics. Everything you keep stays on your device.
The only personal information we ever receive is what you give when you buy a license (see "Buying a license").

## What moodoodle stores, and where

When you press **Keep** (or "Keep image", "Keep all on this page", or **Keep crop**), moodoodle stores in your
browser's local storage (IndexedDB), on your device only:

- the image file you chose to keep, or only the part you cropped, with any edits you made (rotation, flip,
  brightness, contrast, saturation, black & white)
- the address and title of the page it came from, and the image's own address
- the date you kept it, its size, its color palette, and any tags you add
- the credit the page or the image file states for it: creator name and link, license, copyright notice, and
  whether the creator opted out of AI use (you can correct any of these)
- which boards it's in, and small settings such as the board new keeps go into

This is "website content" and "web browsing activity" in Chrome Web Store terms. It is used only to show,
search and export your collection. It is never sent to us or anyone else, never sold, and never used for advertising.

To show the Keep and Crop buttons, moodoodle runs on the pages you visit and notices when your pointer is over an
image. Nothing about a page is stored or sent until you press Keep or Crop. Opening the popup counts the images on
the current page so it can offer "Keep all"; that count is not stored. While you crop, the selection is held in
the browser's temporary session storage and cleared when you keep or cancel.

## Find similar runs on your device

**Find similar** compares images with a small image-recognition model that ships inside the extension and runs
on your computer. moodoodle stores a numeric "fingerprint" of each kept image for this (never for images whose
creator opted out of AI use). No image, fingerprint or address is sent anywhere, and the model is never downloaded
from the internet.

## Buying a license

Downloading your saved and cropped images (as a .zip with credits) and using more than three boards need a
**moodoodle Plus** license. Licenses are sold through **Dodo Payments**, which acts as the merchant of record:

- **At checkout**, Dodo Payments collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details, processes
  the payment and handles taxes. Card details go only to Dodo Payments and its payment processors; we never see them.
  Their privacy policy applies to checkout: https://dodopayments.com/privacy-policy
- **We receive** your name, email address, country, what you bought and when, and your license key, so we can send
  you your license, help you if something goes wrong, and handle refunds. We don't use them for anything else, never
  sell them, and don't send marketing email unless you ask for it.
- **In the extension**, moodoodle stores your license key on your device. To check that the key is valid, it sends
  the key and a device label (for example "Chrome on Linux") to Dodo Payments' license service when you activate it
  and occasionally afterwards. No images, page addresses or collection data are ever included.

We keep purchase records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, then delete them.

## When information leaves your browser

- If you click **Open in Google Lens**: moodoodle opens Google Lens in a new tab with that image's address.
  From then on, Google's privacy policy applies to that page.
- If you have a license: the license check described above.

Nothing else ever leaves your browser.

## Your control and your rights

- Remove any image or board from the gallery at any time.
- Uninstalling moodoodle deletes everything it stored on your device, including your license key on that device.
- If you bought a license, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your purchase information (except what the
  law requires us to keep) by writing to the contact address below. We reply within 30 days.
- If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, we'll tell you in the extension before it takes effect.
