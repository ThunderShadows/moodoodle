good questions

Things people ask.

Is my collection private?

Yes. Everything you keep stays in your browser. kudoodle has no accounts, no servers and no analytics, and Find similar runs on your own computer. See the privacy policy.

Can I use the images I keep?

kudoodle is for personal reference. Each keep shows what the artist allows: Free to reuse, Reuse with conditions, or Reference only when nothing is stated. If you reuse something, the CREDITS.md in your download gives you the credit line.

Why can't I keep a whole page at once on art sites?

Artists on platforms like ArtStation and Cara have asked not to be collected in bulk, so kudoodle doesn't. You can still keep the pieces you love one at a time.

What does "lifetime" mean? Will the price change?

Pay once and Plus is yours, including future updates. The first 20 copies are $9 and the next 60 are $19. Whatever you pay, you never pay again, even when the price goes up later.

What if it's not for me?

Email us within 30 days and you'll get a full refund, no questions asked.

Which browsers does it work on?

Chrome and other Chromium browsers that support Chrome extensions, such as Edge, Brave and Opera.
